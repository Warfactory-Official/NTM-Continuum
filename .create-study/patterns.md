[both] [seen] Real rods: neutron absorbers (boron carbide, hafnium, Ag-In-Cd). Inserting lowers power, withdrawing raises it; withdraw slowly at startup. Scram drops all rods in at once and stops the chain reaction, but decay heat continues for days and still needs cooling. Shutdown margin = enough rod worth to hold the core subcritical. (wikipedia-style control rod page, physicsforums, prop1.org primer) 2026-11-12
[both] [seen] HBM rods: control rod passes 0-100% of flux; GUI sets 25% steps, console any integer; colour groups for remote use; auto rods act on heat. Flux: fuel emits in 4 cardinal directions, fast or slow; moderator (graphite) makes fast into slow; reflector sends flux back; absorber eats all. Rods go between fuel and other flux sources. 1500C in any column = meltdown. (nucleartech.wiki/wiki/RBMK) 2026-11-12
